Privacy

How this demo treats personal information.

This notice covers the concept website only. It is not the privacy notice of BDP Attorneys, and it does not replace one.

Who is responsible here

4th Industrial Solutions built this concept demo. BDP Attorneys did not ask you for information through this site, and the firm does not receive what you type. For a real instruction, use the contact details published at bdplaw.co.za.

What the forms ask for

Intake, booking and the office enquiry ask for a name, email, phone number and a description of a matter, plus the names of other parties if you choose to give them. The portal asks you to select a file. Consent checkboxes record that you have read this notice. On intake, they also record that the story may be read by a language model and that the report may be emailed to 4th Industrial Solutions. The other forms stay in the browser.

Where it goes

The office enquiry and the portal run in your browser. Booking is different: the chosen consultation is stored in this browser so the slot is no longer offered, and the confirmation, with a calendar file, is emailed to 4th Industrial Solutions at info@4irsolutions.co.za. It is not sent to BDP Attorneys and it does not change an attorney’s real diary. The confirmation includes the example fee and whether it was marked paid online, pay at the office, or free. The demo does not operate its own database.

Fees on the diary and the booking page are examples for this demonstration. They are not a published tariff of BDP Attorneys. Pay now opens a checkout page in this browser that looks like a card payment. The card number, expiry and security code are checked on that page and then discarded. They are not emailed or stored. No charge is made. The screen is a demonstration of secure online payment.

A confirmed booking is shown in this browser on the attorney’s calendar and on an example receptionist calendar for Rondebosch or the Garden Route. The calendar file names that attorney and that receptionist, and for this demo it is delivered to info@4irsolutions.co.za. It does not sync to a live Outlook or Google calendar. An attorney letter draft can be emailed to the same demo inbox. It is not sent to the other side.

Smart intake is the exception. The story is sent to a language-model service that does not need an account on this site, so an overview can be drafted. The overview names an area of law, urgency, facts, a plain description of the matter, risks, dates to check, and the questions or documents still needed. That overview, with every field you typed, is emailed to 4th Industrial Solutions at info@4irsolutions.co.za. Reply-to is the address you typed. A printable copy stays in this browser, with a Download PDF report button. If the model does not answer, the same overview is drafted in the browser, in ordinary words. It does not open a file at the firm. Do not put a real identity number in the story.

Why consent is asked

The Protection of Personal Information Act requires a lawful basis before personal information is processed. On this demo the basis is your consent, limited to showing how an enquiry would be handled. You can refuse. The form will not continue without the two required boxes. Refusing has no effect on BDP Attorneys, because the firm does not receive the form.

Your rights, in outline

POPIA gives data subjects rights to know what is held, to ask for correction or deletion, and to object to certain processing. The portal leaves nothing to retrieve after you close the page. An intake overview or a booking confirmation is emailed, so a correction or deletion request for that message can be sent to info@4irsolutions.co.za. The message is kept so it can be delivered. A complaint about a real responsible party can be directed to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

The assistant

The website assistant and the WhatsApp-styled assistant answer from a fixed set of notes based on the firm’s public pages. They do not call an external model with your question, and they are not a legal opinion.
