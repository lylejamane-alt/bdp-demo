The firm

Your success is the measure they publish.

The practice traces its Cape Town story to Honey Attorneys, founded by Dudley Honey in Bloemfontein in 1962 and extended to Cape Town in 1996. In 2013 that Cape Town branch became Brink De Beer and Potgieter Inc., led by George de Beer and Christo Potgieter.

Today the firm describes itself as multi-disciplinary, with personal attention and professional integrity at the centre of the relationship. Clients named as a group on the public site include private individuals, corporates, financial institutions, property developers and retailers.

How the firm says it adds value

  • A strategy that includes the commercial picture

    The firm describes its advice as legal and commercial together, shaped to the matter rather than a template.

  • Risk handled before it becomes a dispute

    Contracts, policies and governance are part of how the firm says it keeps clients out of avoidable trouble.

  • A person who answers

    Published client service is built around a responsive point of contact and regular updates.

  • More than one discipline in the room

    Teams are assembled across practices when a matter does not sit neatly in a single area.

  • Fees discussed in plain terms

    The firm says it looks for practical fee arrangements and cost-conscious advice. This demo does not quote a fee.
